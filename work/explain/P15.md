## Why this matters for the viva specifically
A pre-written answer that's quietly wrong is worse than no answer at all in a viva — if a student is asked "explain
the K pair" and confidently cites MC13, an examiner who checks the actual code will immediately see the mismatch
and lose confidence in everything else the student says, even the parts that *are* right. This is exactly the
category of mistake AGENTS.md/CLAUDE.md's "explain-back" and audit discipline exists to catch before it reaches a
real exam room, not after.
